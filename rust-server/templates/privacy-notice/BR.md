### Study data handling

We collect dialogue and game data from a collaborative two-player game to study communication and human–AI interaction and evaluate AI chatbots. Your partner may be another participant or an AI chatbot. The study is intended for participants aged 18 or older.

The study collects your Prolific ID or other recruitment identifier, live microphone audio, a random study-specific participant ID, game actions and timing, achieved scores, and transcripts of messages. These data come from your participation, microphone input, recruitment information, and records generated during the game. Research records remain linked to your recruitment identifier during the study and are not anonymous during that period.

Parlando does not write participant IP addresses to its research database or corpus exports.

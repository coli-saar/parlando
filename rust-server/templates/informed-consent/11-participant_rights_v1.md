### Participant rights

1. Participation is voluntary. You may decline or stop participating without giving a reason at any time.
2. To request access, correction, or deletion of your data, or to withdraw your consent, email {{study contact email}}. Include your study-specific participant ID or recruitment identifier and the action you would like us to take. Please submit requests concerning your research contributions before the planned anonymization at the end of the study ({{end date}}). Once the data have been anonymized, we can no longer locate your individual contributions by your identity. You can still contact us about any identifiable records that remain, as described in the separately provided privacy notice at {{privacy notice URL}}.

You can use the following email template. Replace the placeholders with your details. For {{the action to take}}, specify, for example, access to your data, correction of specified information, deletion of your data, or withdrawal of consent. You do not need to give a reason for withdrawing consent. You may also use this template to ask about compensation after stopping early, an unavailable partner, or a connection problem; describe what happened so the study team can review your request.

To: {{study contact email}}
Subject: {{study name}} — {{the action to take}}

Dear study team,

I participated in {{study name}} on {{participation date}}.
My study-specific participant ID or recruitment identifier is {{participant identifier}}.

I would like you to {{the action to take}}.
{{Details needed to carry out the request, if applicable}}

Please confirm receipt and let me know the outcome of my request.

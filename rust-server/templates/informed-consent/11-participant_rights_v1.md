Participation is voluntary. You may decline or stop participating without giving a reason. {{HOW_TO_STOP_AND_COMPENSATION_RULES}}

During the two weeks after your participation, contact {{WITHDRAWAL_CONTACT_AND_PROCEDURE}} and provide {{STUDY_ID_OR_RECRUITMENT_IDENTIFIER_PROCEDURE}} to request withdrawal and deletion of your linked research data. After the research team has anonymized the data, it can no longer identify or remove your individual contributions based on your identity.

For two weeks after participating, you may contact {{DATA_REQUEST_CONTACT}} and provide {{STUDY_ID_OR_RECRUITMENT_IDENTIFIER_PROCEDURE}} to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your pseudonymized research data. After anonymization is complete, the research team can no longer identify your individual contributions based on your identity.

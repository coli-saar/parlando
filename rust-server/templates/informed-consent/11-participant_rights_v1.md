### Participant rights

- Participation is voluntary. You may decline or stop participating without giving a reason at any time.
- To access, correct, or delete any provided data, contact the study conductor via email up until two weeks after participation. The email must contain the unique identifier you received for the study and instructions on how you wish your data to be handled. After anonymization of the data, it can no longer identify or remove your individual contributions based on your identity.

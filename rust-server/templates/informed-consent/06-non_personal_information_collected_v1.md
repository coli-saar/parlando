### Collection of non-personal data

This study collects and processes following non-personal data:

- Game interaction data (click actions, time between actions)
- Achieved score
- Study-specific identifiers
- Transcripts of the sent messages

### Processing of non-personal data

The data collected above will be processed in the following ways:

- Game interaction data will be stored without any further processing
- Achieved score data will be stored without any further processing
- Study-specific identifiers will be stored for the duration of the study and then undergo full anonymization. Anonymization will replace all identifiers by randomly generated, unique identifiers.
- Transcripts of the sent messages will be stored for the duration of the study and then undergo full anonymization. Anonymization involves manual screening of all transcripts to identify and delete any personal information.

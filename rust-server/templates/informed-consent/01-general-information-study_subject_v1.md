### Subject of this study

The main purpose of this study is to collect dialogue data from two players that play an interactive game. A core element of this study is the use of speech as the primary means of communication between players, allowing them to simultaenously communicate and interact with the game environment. In addition to data collection, the study will also evaluate the performance of AI chatbots in such collaborative gaming environments; i.e., studying human-AI interaction as well as AI-AI interaction.

### Experiences associated with the participation in the study

When participating in this study, you will be asked to play a two player, collaborative game. The game is set up in a manner that requires collaboration to successfully finish it. In other words, players may act in different environments or receive only partial information that needed to successfully finishing the game. You will be asked to interact with the other player by voice chat and with the game environment via simple click actions. 


### Risks associated with the participation in the study

Participants of the study do not face any risks which are greater than risks they face in general life. All participants agree to adhere to the communication and privacy rules stated below and to treat each other respectfully.

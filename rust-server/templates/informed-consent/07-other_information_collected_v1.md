### Collection of other research data

This study collects and processes following other research data:

- Game interaction data (click actions, time between actions)
- Achieved score
- Study-specific identifiers
- Transcripts of the sent messages

### Processing of other research data

The data collected above will be processed in the following ways:

- Game interaction data will be stored without any further processing
- Achieved score data will be stored without any further processing
- Study-specific identifiers will be stored for the duration of the study and then undergo full anonymization as described in the section "Ensuring the confidentiality of the data".
- Transcripts of the sent messages will be stored for the duration of the study ({{end date}}) and then undergo full anonymization as described in the section "Ensuring the confidentiality of the data".

### Subject of this study

The goal of this study is to collect dialogue data from two players that play an interactive game. The games are set up in a manner that require collaboration to finish them. In other words, different players act in different environments or receive only partial information for successfully finishing the game. Depending on the game, you may either be asked to interact with the other player by either voice chat or messages. 

Please note that players can be humans or AI chatbots.

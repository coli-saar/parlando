### Ensuring the confidentiality of the data

The data will be handled as follows to ensure that any potentially personal data remains confidential.

1. _During the study_: During the study, all collected data will be stored in {{server}} conforming to the {{institute}} guidelines for research data management. Data storage will initially take place in a pseudonymized form.
2. _Upon finishing the game but before closin the study_: After your participation, all records are pseudonymized with a randomly generated study-specific participant ID until the end of the study. During this period, they remain linked to your Prolific ID, allowing the study conductor to locate your contributions when you request access, correction, or deletion.
3. _After the study_:  After the study ends ({{end date}}), the study conductor anonymizes the research data by removing links to recruitment records and reviewing and removing identifying information from the content. Once anonymization is complete, you can no longer be identified from the anonymized data, and your individual contributions can no longer be located for access or deletion based on your identity. After anonymization, the collected data will made publicly available using following license: {{license}}.

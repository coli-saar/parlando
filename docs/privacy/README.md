# Privacy and data protection

Parlando separates platform-level privacy mechanisms from study-specific and
institutional decisions. The platform records consent against a versioned
participant notice, limits exports to defined data shapes, and provides controls
for reviewing and deleting participant data. The deploying institution remains
responsible for the legal basis, retention periods, provider agreements, and
approval of each study.

Use the document that matches your task:

- [Participant Information and Privacy Notice v1.0](participant-information-v1.0.md)
  is the English template shown to prospective participants. Replace every
  placeholder and obtain local approval before use.
- [Consent Items v1.0](consent-items-v1.0.yaml) is the corresponding
  machine-readable consent configuration. Its version and wording must remain
  aligned with the published participant notice.
- [Unterlage zur datenschutzrechtlichen Plattformbewertung](datenschutz-pruefvorlage.md)
  supports institutional or data-protection-officer assessment of a self-hosted
  Parlando installation.
- [Technische Datenschutzfunktionen](datenschutz-funktionen.md) explains the
  privacy mechanisms implemented by the platform and identifies responsibilities
  that remain outside the platform core.

The [security ground rules and threat model](../security-ground-rules.md) define
the security assumptions on which these materials rely. Operational guidance
for exports, monitoring, and participant-data deletion is in
[Data and monitoring](../data-and-monitoring.md).
